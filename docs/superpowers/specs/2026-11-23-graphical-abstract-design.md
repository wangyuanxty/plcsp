# Graphical Abstract Design

**Goal:** Make a one-page companion to the HMAGRA manuscript for author review before submission to Computers & Industrial Engineering.

**Approaches:** Reuse the cross-workshop route from Fig. 1(a) and place it beside a compact method summary and the main results. Use this approach instead of shrinking complete manuscript figures, which retain more detail than can fit legibly at 130 mm.

**Design:** Read left to right: two fleets coordinate a job transfer through load/unload stations; HMAGRA uses a high-level rule selector to guide the joint operation, machine, and AGV decisions; the policy lowers the mean objective by approximately 19%–37% relative to the strongest DRL baseline in each of five test families. A secondary line reports the reversal with genetic search: lower mean objectives for genetic search in the two smaller families and for HMAGRA in the three larger families.

**Deliverables:** One local HTML slide and one single-page landscape PDF. The HTML print canvas is at least 1328 px wide; the PDF must be legible when scaled to 130 mm wide. Use original manuscript artwork and verified results. No generative image tools, external uploads, or edits to the article. Confirm the rights of reused artwork before submission.
